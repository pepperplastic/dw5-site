David Weiss · Delray Beach, Florida

I build and run small consumer businesses. Most of them are about gold and jewelry. A couple I just couldn't leave alone.

The main one is Snappy Gold, a mail-in gold and jewelry buyer I started in early 2026. People photograph what they've got, get an honest number, and ship it to me on a prepaid label. I open every package myself. When the piece deserves more than melt value, it goes to Beached Gold, where I resell estate and vintage jewelry to people who'll actually wear it.

Before this I spent six years building Par Nasa, a medical-supply company, from a spreadsheet to eight figures in revenue and a sale in 2021. Before that: marketing at ModernAd Media, a stint at Morgan Stanley, and a South Florida magazine I co-founded out of college. I like businesses where the customer can tell within a week whether you kept your word.

On the side: BoxingFix, because I've never stopped watching boxing; wikiconflict, a plain-language history of the Israeli–Palestinian conflict; and FeudMe, where two people argue and an AI keeps score.

I work with a handful of advertisers and partners whose offers I'd recommend to a customer standing in front of me. If that's you, write.
