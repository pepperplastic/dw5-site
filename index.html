DW5 LLC operates consumer businesses and content properties in jewelry, precious metals, and media.

We build and run our own properties rather than services for others. The largest is Snappy Gold, a licensed mail-in buyer of gold and jewelry that has handled well over a thousand consumer inquiries and hundreds of inbound shipments since launching in early 2026. Alongside it: an estate-jewelry resale storefront, a boxing news site, a reference site on Middle East history, and two small consumer web applications.

Because our audience arrives with a specific, valuable intent — they own jewelry or precious metals and are deciding what to do with it — we work with a small number of advertisers whose offers are relevant to that moment. See Partnerships for how that works, or Properties for the register.
