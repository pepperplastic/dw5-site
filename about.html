About DW5 LLC

DW5 LLC is a Florida limited liability company based in Delray Beach. It is owned and run by its founder, David Weiss, who operates each property directly.

David Weiss

David founded Par Nasa in 2015, a medical-supply e-commerce company built on inefficiencies in that supply chain, and led it to eight figures in annual revenue and a sale in 2021. Before that he was Vice President of Marketing and Product at ModernAd Media and Acquinity Interactive in Deerfield Beach, where he also supported the launch of a boxing and hip-hop live-entertainment venture, and earlier a financial advisor at Morgan Stanley. He co-founded and edited Jimmy Magazine, a South Florida monthly, after graduating from Florida Atlantic University's University Scholars Program in 2007.

He holds a Florida real estate license and operates Snappy Gold as a registered secondhand precious-metals dealer under Florida law, which requires identity verification and transaction records on every purchase.
