Working with advertisers

We participate in affiliate programs as a publisher, and we run direct partnerships with advertisers whose offers fit the audience described on the Audience page. In both cases the offer has to be one we would recommend to a customer standing in front of us.

Placements

  • Email — featured mentions in messages to opted-in Snappy Gold subscribers, including the sequences sent to people who requested a valuation but did not sell.
  • On-site — contextual placements on snappy.gold and beachedgold.com at the points where a visitor is weighing alternatives, such as a declined offer or an item outside what we buy.
  • Editorial — mentions on BoxingFix where relevant.

Tracking

We support standard affiliate links and, where the advertiser offers it, server-to-server conversion postbacks. Our own systems already report conversions server-side to advertising and affiliate platforms, so we can work with click IDs, sub-IDs, and postback URLs without additional engineering on the advertiser's side.

What we ask

  • Offers that are genuinely useful to someone selling or valuing jewelry, precious metals, or collectibles.
  • Clear terms: what counts as a conversion, the payout, and the attribution window.
  • Reasonable payment terms; we prefer monthly.

Get in touch

Write to partnerships@dw5.llc with the program or offer, the payout structure, and any creative or tracking requirements. Network invitations to the same address.
