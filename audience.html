Who our properties reach

Most of our audience comes through Snappy Gold, and it is defined less by demographics than by a moment: someone who owns jewelry or precious metals and is deciding whether to sell, what it is worth, or how to handle an inheritance.

Geography
United States, all fifty states; Florida over-represented.
Age and gender
Predominantly 35 and older; roughly two-thirds women.
Intent
Active — they have photographed a specific piece and requested a valuation.
Email list
Over two thousand opt-in subscribers who registered on snappy.gold; contacted by email and SMS with their consent.
Content properties
Smaller, interest-driven audiences: boxing readers on BoxingFix, students and researchers on wikiconflict.

Categories where we can be useful to an advertiser

  • Jewelry and diamond resale, auction, and appraisal services
  • Precious-metals dealers and bullion
  • Jewelry insurance and valuation
  • Estate, probate, and downsizing services
  • Collectibles — coins, watches
  • Sports and boxing media, for BoxingFix

We do not sell or rent subscriber data. Advertisers reach the audience through placements we control.
